
### AI-Driven Government Scheme Matching Platform for Marginalized Entrepreneurs

Saksham AI is an intelligent web platform designed to help marginalized and underserved entrepreneurs discover relevant government schemes, subsidies, financial assistance, and support programs.

Instead of searching through multiple government portals and documents, users can provide their basic information and business requirements, and Saksham AI helps identify schemes that may be relevant to them.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many marginalized entrepreneurs are unaware of the government schemes and financial support available to them.

Common challenges include:

- Difficulty finding relevant government schemes
- Complex eligibility requirements
- Information spread across multiple portals
- Lack of personalized scheme recommendations
- Difficulty understanding government policies and benefits

Saksham AI aims to make this process simpler, faster, and more accessible.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Saksham AI uses an AI-driven matching approach to analyze user information and identify potentially suitable government schemes.

### How it works

```text
User Information
       ↓
Profile & Business Details
       ↓
AI-Based Scheme Matching
       ↓
Eligibility Analysis
       ↓
Relevant Government Schemes
       ↓
Benefits & Application Information

✨ Key Features

🤖 AI-Powered Scheme Matching

Matches entrepreneurs with government schemes based on their profile, business type, requirements, and eligibility information.

🎯 Personalized Recommendations

Provides scheme recommendations instead of requiring users to manually search through large amounts of information.

📋 Eligibility Analysis

Helps users understand whether they may meet the requirements of a particular scheme.

💰 Benefits Information

Displays important information about available financial assistance, subsidies, loans, and other benefits.

🔎 Scheme Discovery

Allows users to explore and search available government schemes.

📱 User-Friendly Interface

Designed with a simple and accessible interface so that users with different levels of technical knowledge can navigate the platform.
